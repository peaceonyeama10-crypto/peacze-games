THE FUTURE OF CROWN NETWORK

Our plans.
Our next chapter.

CROWN NETWORK is a growing project built around games, creators, live entertainment, and community. Here's what we're working toward and what we hope to build in the future.

What we plan to build

These are our goals for the website and community. Features may change as the project develops.

PLAN 01

🎮 More Games

Expand our game selection and make it easier for visitors to discover experiences, join games, and explore what's available through CROWN NETWORK.

PLAN 02

📱 TikTok LIVE Integration

Continue developing ways to connect TikTok LIVE activity with supported games, including viewer participation, interactive features, and live community experiences.

PLAN 03

👑 Creator Features

Explore tools and features that help creators connect their streams with games and make their live content more interactive. Availability will depend on what we can successfully develop.

PLAN 04

🌐 A Bigger Community

Build a welcoming community where players and creators can connect, share ideas, give feedback, and help shape the future of the project.

PLAN 05

⚡ Website Improvements

Improve the website's design, performance, navigation, and overall experience, with more useful pages and features as CROWN NETWORK grows.

PLAN 06

🚀 New Ideas

Test new game modes, community events, interactive features, and other ideas. Not every idea is guaranteed to become a finished feature, but we're always looking for ways to improve.
